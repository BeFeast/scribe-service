What Scribe is

Scribe is a private, invite-only service that turns a video or audio link you submit into a transcript and a summary. Accounts are created by an operator; there is no public sign-up.

What we store

  • Account data — your email address, display name and the identifier issued by our authentication provider. Passwords are never stored by Scribe.
  • What you submit — the URLs you send, and any media you upload, together with the resulting transcript, summary, title, tags and processing metadata (timings, cost, error messages).
  • Operational logs — request and job logs used to run and debug the service.

Who else processes it

Running a job means sending data to a small number of processors:

  • Clerk — authentication (sign-in, session tokens, your email and profile fields).
  • Google — only if you choose “Continue with Google”. Scribe receives your email address, name and profile picture; nothing is written back to your Google account.
  • Transcription and summarisation providers — the extracted audio is transcribed on a rented GPU instance, and the transcript text is sent to a large-language-model provider to produce the summary. Which provider is used depends on the operator's configuration at the time of the job.
  • Object storage — uploaded media is kept in a private bucket when the upload feature is used.

Scribe does not sell your data, does not serve advertising, and does not use your transcripts to train models.

Retention and deletion

Transcripts and summaries are kept until you or the operator delete them. Intermediate audio is temporary and removed after a job completes. To have your account and its content deleted, write to the contact address below.

Sharing

Content is private to your account unless you create a share link, which makes that single transcript or summary readable by anyone holding the link until you revoke it.
